# Plan 12-03 — Parity extractor fixes: delta-records + per-turn workspace fixtures (ACP-07 evidence leg, D-04)

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer driving ass-guard from an ACP editor, **I want** every tool in the captured catalog
to execute for real with capture-pinned result forms, and turn behavior guarded by a behavioral-eval
regression net, **so that** the product machinery is complete — the model never hits an unimplemented
dead end mid-task.

**This plan's slice:** the evidence half of ACP-07's re-pin story. The 2026-08-16 drift report
decomposed the from-rollout A/B's 1/13 failure into harness artifacts: 6 turns with EMPTY
expected-calls (`ExtractTurnsFromRollout` cannot reconstruct tool_use from `messagesKind=delta`
records) and 4 turns of workspace-state pollution (replays mutate a shared scratch, so later turns
read what earlier replays already edited). D-04 locks both fixes in Phase-12 scope. This plan kills
both classes at the extractor/harness level so that when plan 12-05's re-record produces a fresh
qualifying capture, the from-rollout A/B measures PROFILE divergence instead of harness noise — and
the same fixed extractor is reusable evidence machinery for the eval net (plan 12-08).

  re-pin leg (plan 12-05 consumes this plan's extractor on its fresh capture).

  there too — strictly an artifact-reduction, asserted by its tests staying green).
