<objective>
Make provider request rejection real: today a 400 "prompt is too long" body is silently drained as non-SSE lines and surfaces as an empty end_turn (RESEARCH Pitfall 1, verified at streaming.go:230-233 and :497-501). PAR-01's overflow retry-once recovery cannot exist until overflow errors reach the turn loop. This plan adds the non-2xx status check to the streaming path and the IsOverflow message-class predicate — the foundation 19-04's recovery branch consumes.

Purpose: overflow detection is criterion 3's prerequisite; landing it first (the RESEARCH's recommended wave 1) means the retry work in 19-04 builds on errors that are already real.
Output: status check + error-chunk emission in Stream, IsOverflow predicate, both pinned by tests.
</objective>
